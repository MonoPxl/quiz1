A FEW MOMENTS

Ambon through time.

Open a chapter to see how a few milestones connect with the city's story.

  1. 1575
    Early foundations

    The Government of Ambon uses 1575 as the city's birth year, linked to early settlements around the Portuguese fort at Honipopu.

  2. 1605
    The Dutch period begins

    Ambon's colonial history shifted when the Dutch took over the Portuguese fort, later known as Benteng Victoria.

  3. 1817
    Teenage rebel against the Dutch

    One of Ambon's most prominent monuments honors Martha Christina Tiahahu, an Ambonese guerrilla leader who fought fiercely against the colonial military in 1817 starting when she was just 17 years old.

  4. 20th century
    A changing regional city

    Ambon developed as an administrative and commercial center while remaining closely connected to the surrounding islands and maritime life.
